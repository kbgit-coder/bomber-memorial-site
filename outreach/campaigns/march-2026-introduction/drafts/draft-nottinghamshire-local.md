Subject: Bomber Command Memorial Register — a local heritage project you might find interesting

Dear [Organisation Name],

My name is Keith Binley, and I'm based in Farnsfield, Nottinghamshire. I'm writing to let you know about a community heritage project I run called the Bomber Command Memorial Register (bombermemorial.co.uk).

The Register documents WW2 bomber crash site memorials across the UK — 208 so far — with photographs, crew details, GPS coordinates, and condition assessments. As a Farnsfield resident, one of the memorials closest to my heart is the Halifax MZ519 memorial here in the village, which commemorates the crew of a 1652 Heavy Conversion Unit aircraft that crashed on 11th November 1944.

Nottinghamshire has several bomber crash site memorials, and I'd like to make sure our records for the county are as complete and accurate as possible. If you're aware of any memorials we might have missed, or if you spot anything that needs correcting on our existing entries, I'd be very grateful to hear from you.

The Register is entirely free and community-driven — no advertising, no subscriptions, no commercial agenda. It exists because these memorials matter, and the stories behind them deserve to be accessible to anyone who wants to find them.

If there's an opportunity to share the Register with your members, community, or network, that would be wonderful. I'm also happy to give a short talk or presentation about the project if that would be of interest.
