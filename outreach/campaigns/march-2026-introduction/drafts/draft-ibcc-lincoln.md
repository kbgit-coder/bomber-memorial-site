Subject: Bomber Command Memorial Register — complementing the IBCC Digital Archive

Dear IBCC team,

I'm Keith Binley, and I run the Bomber Command Memorial Register (bombermemorial.co.uk) — a free community project that maps and documents WW2 bomber crash site memorials across the UK. We currently record 208 memorials with photographs, condition data, GPS coordinates, and crew information.

I've long admired the work you do at the International Bomber Command Centre, particularly the digital archive. Your archive is the definitive resource for crew records and personal stories. The Memorial Register approaches the same history from a different angle — we focus on the physical crash sites and the memorials that mark them. Where your records tell the story of the people, ours tell the story of the places.

I think there's a natural opportunity for the two resources to link to each other. Many of our memorial entries reference crew members who appear in your archive, and visitors to your site researching a particular crew might want to see the memorial that now stands where their aircraft came down.

We'd be very open to any form of collaboration — whether that's simple reciprocal linking, data sharing, or even displaying IBCC archive links directly on our memorial pages. Our data is structured (JSON schema on GitHub) so integration would be straightforward from a technical standpoint.

I'd welcome a conversation about how we might work together. No pressure at all — even just knowing you're aware of the Register would be valuable.

With kind regards,
