I'm Keith Binley, and I run the Bomber Command Memorial Register (bombermemorial.co.uk) — a free, community-driven project that documents WW2 bomber crash site memorials across the UK. We have 208 memorials recorded so far.

I'm reaching out because I think there's a natural opportunity for our projects to cross-reference and link to each other. Many of the crew members commemorated at our memorial sites will appear in your records, and visitors researching specific individuals or units would benefit from being able to move between our resources.

A bit about how the Register is built, since I think this matters for a digital collaboration:

- **Structured data** — Every memorial is stored as structured JSON with a documented schema
- **GitHub repository** — Our data is openly available on GitHub, making integration straightforward
- **Unique identifiers** — Each memorial has a unique ID, along with OS grid references, GPS coordinates, and What3Words locations
- **Crew records** — We record crew names, ranks, and service numbers where known, which could serve as cross-reference keys

We'd be interested in exploring any of the following:
- Reciprocal linking between matching records
- Embedding links to your platform on our memorial pages (and vice versa)
- Bulk data exchange for cross-referencing

The Register is non-commercial and open. We're happy to work with whatever format or process suits your platform best.

Would you be open to an initial conversation about whether a link-up makes sense? No commitment needed — I'd just like to explore the possibilities.
