Subject: Bomber Command Memorial Register — we already link to you, let's make it official

Dear Aircrew Remembered team,

I'm Keith Binley, and I run the Bomber Command Memorial Register (bombermemorial.co.uk). I wanted to reach out because your site is already one of our most valued sources.

A number of our 208 memorial entries link directly to Aircrew Remembered pages for crew details. When someone visits a memorial page on our Register and wants to know more about the crew who were lost, we point them to you. Your depth of research on individual aircrew is simply unmatched.

I'd like to propose that we formalise this relationship. At the moment, we link to you but there's no reciprocal connection. I think visitors to Aircrew Remembered who are researching a particular crew might find it valuable to see that a physical memorial exists at the crash site — with photographs, a map, and a What3Words location so they could actually visit.

What I have in mind is straightforward:
- We continue linking to your crew pages from our memorial entries (and can add more)
- You could link back to our memorial pages where a relevant memorial exists
- We could coordinate on data accuracy — if either of us spots an error, we let the other know

The Register is a free, community-driven project with no commercial element. Our data is openly structured and available on GitHub. I'm not looking to duplicate your work — quite the opposite. Your crew research and our site-level detail are naturally complementary.

Would you be open to a conversation about linking up? Even informally would be a great start.

With appreciation for the work you do,
Keith Binley
