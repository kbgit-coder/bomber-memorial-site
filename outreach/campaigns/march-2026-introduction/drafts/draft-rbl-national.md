Subject: Bomber Command Memorial Register — connecting with local RBL branches

Dear Royal British Legion team,

I'm writing to introduce the Bomber Command Memorial Register (bombermemorial.co.uk), a free community project that documents WW2 bomber crash site memorials across the UK. We currently have 208 memorials mapped and recorded, each with photographs, crew details, What3Words locations, and condition assessments.

I wanted to reach out because a number of our memorial pages already include details of nearby Royal British Legion branches, particularly where branches have been involved in memorial maintenance or remembrance events. We think the Register could be a useful resource for local branches, and we'd love your help connecting with them.

Many of these memorials sit in rural or hard-to-find locations. Every entry on the Register includes a What3Words address and grid reference so that visitors — including wreath-laying parties — can find the exact spot. Several memorials have been adopted by local communities, and we'd like to see more of that.

The Register is entirely community-driven. There's no commercial element — just a desire to make sure these memorials are remembered and looked after. It's built on openly available data, and anyone can suggest corrections or additions.

Would it be possible to share the Register with your county and branch network? Even a mention in an internal bulletin would help us reach the volunteers who care most about these sites. We're happy to provide any materials or information that would be useful.

Thank you for everything the Legion does to keep remembrance alive.

With best wishes,
