## Wressle — Intake Plantation (TWO memorials, one location)

### 1. Halifax JD151 (EY-M), 78 Sqn — crashed 11 Aug 1943
- Took off RAF Breighton 11 Aug 1943 21:30, target Nuremberg
- On return, engines failed (fuel exhaustion) on approach for priority landing at Breighton; crashed short of runway (poster says "Bowthorpe Hall, South Driffield, Selby" — reconcile location naming during research)
- Killed: Sgt H Binns (buried Southport (Birkdale) Cemetery), F/Sgt W J Brown (Liverpool (Allerton) Cemetery), Sgt T Gillicker (Warrington Cemetery)
- Survived: F/Sgt Jenkinson + Sgt T Eyeles (minor injuries), F/Lt O'Neill + Sgt Barger (hospitalised)
- **Community connection:** Hazel King (Facebook) — her father was aboard and survived; lost his friend Harry Binns and kept his photo for the rest of his life. The pilot carried the what-if of not diverting. Living-memory material — handle with care, do not quote her verbatim without permission.
- Squadron EY code = 78 Sqn — verify

### 2. Halifax DT524 — crashed 7 Sep 1943
- Training flight from RAF Riccall (1658 HCU likely — verify)
- Memorial at same location, Intake Plantation, Wressle
- Crew details: research needed

## Uelsen, Lower Saxony, Germany — Wellington BK313 (KO-B), 115 Sqn
- Memorial stone, plaque and information board at rural crash site
- Departed Mildenhall 06/10/1942 19:01, Ops Osnabrück
- Intercepted at 4,000m near Rhine by nightfighter Lt Hans Krause (Stab I./NJG3), shot down 23:22 — his 2nd Abschuss
- Crashed at Uelsen, all five crew killed
- Sources cited by poster: Chorley BCL Vol 3; Boiten NCA The Early Years Part 3
- Photos: Robert de Vries — permission was to the Facebook poster, NOT to BMR. No photo use until our own permission is granted.

## Kortrijk/Bissegem, Belgium — Three Lancasters monument (seed detail for existing capture)
- Monument on Wevelgemsevoetweg, along the Lys, Bissegem — commemorates THREE Lancasters lost over Kortrijk region night 20–21 Jul 1944 (raid on Kortrijk rail infrastructure): W4967, LM101, PD205
- Lancaster I W4967 SR-P, 101 Sqn: presumably hit by nightfighter (possibly Karl Beyer, 1./NJG 4), crashed near Carmelite monastery, Kortrijk
- Crew (mixed RAF/RCAF): F/O Sydney E.F.H. Smith RCAF pilot † (Wevelgem Municipal Cemetery); P/O Robert A. Simpson RCAF bomb aimer † (Adegem Canadian War Cemetery); P/O Carlton H. Woelfle RCAF AG/WOp † (Wevelgem); Sgt Hugh McGuigan RAF AG † (Wevelgem); WO2 G.A. Noble RCAF nav — POW
- Source: Facebook poster Evenepoel Philippe, 23 Sep. Verify against CWGC + Chorley; research LM101 + PD205 crews for the record

## Mechelen-Leest, Antwerpen, Belgium — Halifax JD259 (NP-R), 158 Sqn
- Memorial stone and plaque at rural crash site
- Departed Lissett 21/06/1943 23:09, Ops Mülheim
- Intercepted at 5,200m above Leest by Oblt Wilhelm Telge (Stab II./NJG1), shot down 02:30 on 22/06/43 — his 7th Abschuss
- Crashed at Leest, all seven crew killed
- Sources cited by poster: Chorley BCL Vol 4; Boiten NCA 1943 Part 2
- Photos: Stvn Volckaerts — permission was to the poster, NOT to BMR. No photo use until our own permission granted.

## Revigny-sur-Ornain / Contrisson, Meuse, France — Lancaster LM551 (JO-G), 463 (RAAF) Sqn
- Newly dedicated information panel, rural location
- Departed Waddington 18/07/1944 22:53, Ops Revigny; crashed into Canal de la Marne au Rhin near Contrisson, all seven crew killed, 19/07/44
- Two competing nightfighter claims: Oblt Ernst-Georg Drünkler (1./NJG5, 01:45, 19th Abschuss) OR Hptm Hubert Rauh (Stab II./NJG4, ~01:25, 19th Abschuss) — record should present both, per sources
- Sources cited by poster: Chorley BCL Vol 5; Boiten NCA 1944 Part 3
- Memorial designed and installed by Ben Howson — photos his, permission was to poster, NOT BMR. Howson is a contact-worthy memorial builder.

## Kortrijk PD205 correction/enrichment (for 3-Lancasters record)
- PD205 (WS-H) was No. 9 Sqn RAF, departed RAF Bardney 20/07/1944 23:22, target Kortrijk railway yards
- Shot down by FLAK (1./Flak-Regiment 53), NOT nightfighter; crashed Bissegem ~00:57 21 Jul 1944; all 7 crew killed
- Source: HLN.be article (80 jaar geleden werd Kortrijk...) via poster Evenepoel Philippe

## Herwijnen / Fort Asperen, Gelderland, Netherlands — Mosquito DZ354 (GB-C), 105 Sqn
- Relatively new information panel, rural location
- Departed Marham 12/12/1943 16:52, Ops Essen
- Intercepted at 8,300m(?) 6km N of Herwijnen by Hptm Manfred Meurer (Stab I./NJG1), shot down 19:25 — his 59th Abschuss
- Crashed at Fort Asperen near Herwijnen, both crew killed (Mosquito, 2 crew)
- Sources cited by poster: Chorley BCL Vol 4; Boiten NCA 1943 Part 3
- Photos: Reg Mulder — permission to poster, NOT BMR

## Beetley (All Saints Church), Norfolk, UK — Wellington BJ764 (KW-?), 425 (RCAF) Sqn
- Memorial tablet in the village church
- Departed Dishforth 10/11/1942 17:33, Ops Hamburg
- Believed damaged over occupied territory; two crew had already bailed out into captivity (POW)
- Crashed 23:58 attempting emergency landing at nearby Swanton Morley; remaining four crew killed
- Source cited by poster: Chorley BCL Vol 3
